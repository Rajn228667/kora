# Privacy Policy — KORA

_Last updated: 2025-01-01_

## 1. Data we collect

- **Account data**: phone number, name, profile photo.
- **Order data**: delivery addresses, order contents, payment method tokens (we never store card data — payments are processed by Kaspi).
- **Location**: precise geolocation is collected only while a delivery is being placed (customer) or fulfilled (courier), with your explicit permission.
- **Device data**: push tokens, app version, diagnostics.

## 2. How we use data

We use the data to fulfil orders, route couriers, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the service.

## 3. Sharing

We share order details with the store fulfilling the order, and delivery coordinates with the assigned courier. We do not sell personal data.

## 4. Retention & deletion

You may request account deletion in Profile → Security → Delete account. Orders history is anonymised after deletion as required by Kazakhstan law.
